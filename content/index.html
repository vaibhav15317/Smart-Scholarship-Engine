What are the features

Student profile

Describe yourself once

Education level, category, state, family income, achievements and the documents you already have.

Scholarship explorer

Search and filter schemes

Browse a starter list of Karnataka and central schemes, ranked by how well they match your profile.

My shortlist

Save what looks promising

Keep good options in one place while you check eligibility and application dates.

Official portals

Go straight to the source

Each scheme links to Karnataka SSP, the National Scholarship Portal or AICTE, so you can confirm the rules and apply.

Live preview

See how matching works

A small browser version of the matcher, searching a live database of central and state schemes.
